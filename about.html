About TactiFF

TactiFF is a free gaming sensitivity generator and calibration tool designed to help players find personalized sensitivity settings.

What is TactiFF?

TactiFF combines device information, player profile, drag calibration, aim testing and touch performance settings to create a personalized starting sensitivity profile.

How TactiFF Works

Players can enter their device details, choose their finger setup and playstyle, perform a drag calibration test and adjust performance settings before generating their sensitivity profile.

Why TactiFF?

The goal of TactiFF is to make sensitivity customization easier, faster and more accessible for gamers without requiring complicated manual calculations.

Free to Use

TactiFF is designed to provide useful gaming tools that players can access easily from their browser.
