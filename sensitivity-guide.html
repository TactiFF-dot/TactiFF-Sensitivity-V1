Gaming Sensitivity Guide

Finding comfortable sensitivity settings can make aiming, camera movement, and touch control easier. TactiFF helps players create a personalized starting sensitivity profile based on their device and preferences.

What Is Gaming Sensitivity?

Sensitivity controls how much your aim or camera moves when you move your finger or input device. Higher sensitivity generally allows faster movement, while lower sensitivity can provide slower and more controlled movement.

How to Find Your Sensitivity

There is no single sensitivity setting that is perfect for every player. Your device, screen size, touch response, refresh rate, controls, and personal playstyle can all affect what feels comfortable.

Tip: Start with a balanced setting and make small adjustments instead of changing sensitivity dramatically at once.

Why Device Information Matters

Different devices can have different screen sizes, refresh rates, touch response characteristics, and performance levels. Considering your device can help you choose a more comfortable starting point.

Drag Calibration

Drag calibration can help you understand how your finger movement translates into on-screen movement. A consistent drag can make it easier to evaluate whether your sensitivity feels too fast or too slow.

Aim Testing

Aim testing provides a simple way to evaluate your control after changing sensitivity. Test your settings for a short period and make small changes based on how they feel.

High vs Low Sensitivity

Higher Sensitivity

Higher sensitivity can make quick camera movement easier, but it may also feel harder to control for some players.

Lower Sensitivity

Lower sensitivity can provide slower and more controlled movement, but larger finger movements may be required.

Best Approach

Instead of looking for one universal "best" sensitivity, focus on finding a setting that feels consistent and comfortable for your own device and playstyle.
